//faq

Questions, answered.

Who is EstaMatch for?

Any business that sells through a sales team and works leads through a pipeline.

What can the AI assistant do?

It tells salespeople who to call, adds leads, changes status and books follow ups. Managers see the team pipeline and stuck deals. Owners get business summaries.

Can salespeople see each other's leads?

No. Salespeople see only their own leads. Owners and managers see more, based on their role.

How do AI calls and Gmail outreach work?

The AI agent calls new leads, asks about budget, timeline and intent, and marks them Hot, Warm or Cold. Gmail campaigns send a personal email to every lead on your list from your own address, and replies come back into the pipeline.

Is the Meta ads assistant live?

It is rolling out now. Meta Lead Ads integration is available as a setup add on.

How much does EstaMatch cost?

Pricing depends on your team size and setup. Contact our sales team for a quote.
